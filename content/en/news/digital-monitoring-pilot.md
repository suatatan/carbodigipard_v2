## Pilot Implementation Launch

The pilot implementation of the Digital Carbon Monitoring Platform — one of the project's key outputs — commenced in September 2024. In the first phase, 3 pilot farms in Turkey and 2 in Poland were selected.

## Platform Features

The developed platform provides the following core functions:

- Real-time carbon emission tracking
- Automated recording of agricultural activities
- Simplified reporting interface for farmers
- Carbon credit calculation module

## Data Collection Process

Data collected from farmers during the pilot implementation will be shared with project partners in the form of monthly reports. The process will continue until March 2025.

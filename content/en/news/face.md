🌱 Face-to-Face Survey Activities Continue Within the CarboDigIPARD Project!

The face-to-face survey activities carried out within the framework of the CarboDigIPARD Project, coordinated by our Institution, are continuing at full speed🤝
Through these field surveys, we aim to assess the current level of awareness regarding carbon footprints in IPARD-supported investments, identify the needs of beneficiaries and stakeholders, and shape project activities accordingly.
The data collected through these interviews will provide valuable input for the development of digital tools, training materials, and awareness-raising activities aimed at reducing carbon emissions and promoting sustainable resource management.
We would like to thank all project partners, beneficiaries, PCU Coordinators and ARDSI experts whose support and cooperation contribute to the successful implementation of this project.
🌍 Working together for a more sustainable, climate-friendly, and resilient agricultural sector.
🌱 Measuring Carbon Footprints, Shaping a Sustainable Future Together.

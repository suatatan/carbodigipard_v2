## Technical Workshop: Bologna 2024

The first technical workshop of the CARBODIGIPARD project was held on 20-21 May 2024 at the University of Bologna. The workshop focused on carbon measurement methodologies under Work Package 2.

## Workshop Agenda

The following topics were discussed throughout the workshop:

- Comparison of ISO 14064 and GHG Protocol
- Review of field-level carbon measurement tools
- Digital data collection methods
- Integration of stakeholder feedback into methodology

## Outcomes and Next Steps

A joint methodology draft was prepared at the end of the workshop. This draft will form the basis for the digital tools developed under WP3. The next technical meeting is planned for September 2024 in Warsaw.

Within the scope of the **Erasmus+ KA220-VET programme**, we successfully held our **Transnational Project Meeting** under the CARBODIGIPARD Project.

During the meeting and workshop sessions, we conducted comprehensive discussions and trainings on:

- 🌱 Strategies to reduce environmental impacts in agriculture
- 📊 The importance of carbon footprint and footprint methodologies
- 🌍 The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), its implications for the agriculture and livestock sectors, and Türkiye's roadmap within the CBAM framework
- 📘 The GHG Protocol and carbon footprint calculations in the food and livestock sectors

## Project Kick-Off Meeting

The CARBODIGIPARD project was officially launched on 15 February 2024 at Ankara University with the Kick-off Meeting. Six partner institutions from six European countries convened to discuss project management, work packages, and the working schedule in detail.

## Key Topics Covered

- Establishment of project coordination and management structure
- Formation of technical working groups
- Approval of first-period milestones
- Discussion of data collection methodology

## Participating Institutions

Partners from the Netherlands, Italy, Poland, Germany, and Spain participated alongside coordinating institution Ankara University. 

The project aims to develop innovative methods for measuring, monitoring, and reducing the carbon footprint in agriculture and industrial sectors using digital tools.

## Module Overview

This training module has been prepared for producers and professionals working in the agricultural sector.

### Topics Covered

- What is a carbon footprint and why does it matter?
- Greenhouse gas emissions from agricultural sources
- Core principles of the GHG Protocol (Scope 1, 2, 3)
- Field-level calculation steps
- Documentation and reporting requirements

### Target Audience

- Farmers and farm managers
- Agricultural advisors
- Food supply chain managers

You may review the material at your own pace. A short assessment question is provided at the end of each section.

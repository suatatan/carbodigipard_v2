## Platform User Guide

This guide explains the core features and usage steps of the CARBODIGIPARD Digital Carbon Monitoring Platform.

### Sections

1. **Account Creation and Login** — Steps for first access to the platform
2. **Creating a Farm Profile** — Entering business information
3. **Activity Data Entry** — Recording daily agricultural activities
4. **Reporting Module** — Monthly and annual carbon reports
5. **Carbon Credit Calculation** — Tracking credit scores

### Technical Requirements

The platform runs in all modern browsers and requires an internet connection.
